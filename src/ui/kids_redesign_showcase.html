🎨 Kids Random Animations & Delighters Feature Suite

👀 Googly-Eye Mouse Tracking

Characters, buttons, and logos have pupils that smoothly track the cursor or finger.

🫧 Ambient Poppable Bubbles

Translucent soap bubbles float up at random times; kids can tap them to hear a satisfying pop!

🦊 Peek-a-boo Animals

Random animals peek out from screen corners every 15–30s to wink, wave, and duck back.

🎹 Web Audio Toy Synthesizer

Zero external sound files needed! Generates real-time cartoon boings, xylophone chimes, and pops.

🪅 Moral Celebration Pinata

At the end of a fable, a swinging pinata appears that kids tap 3 times to burst out golden stars!

🧲 Zero-G Sticker Canvas

Kids earn animal stickers as rewards and can stick, drag, and flick them anywhere on the screen.
